Designed for daily dispensary safety.

Every feature focuses on eliminating stock waste and preventing expired medication from being handed to patients.

Batch & Lot Lineage

Every incoming shipment is recorded with its unique batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date, purchase cost, and supplier. No aggregate guesswork.

FEFO Dispensing

First-Expiring, First-Out logic guides staff to dispense batches nearing expiration first, preventing older stock from being forgotten behind newer boxes.

Automatic Quarantine

Batches that cross their expiry date are immediately removed from available stock in the database, locking them against accidental dispensing.

How it works

A streamlined workflow from delivery receiving to customer dispensing.

1
Stock In with Lot Details

When suppliers deliver stock, receive items with their exact lot number, unit purchase price, and expiry date. Multiple batches of the same medicine sit side-by-side cleanly.

2
Continuous Expiry Monitoring

The system categorizes batches into 30-day, 15-day, and 7-day alert horizons. Near-expiry batches can be returned to suppliers for credit before return windows expire.

3
Safe FEFO Dispensing & Audit Logs

During customer sales, the dispenser allocates from the earliest-expiring viable batch. Every transaction is permanently recorded with timestamps and operator IDs.
